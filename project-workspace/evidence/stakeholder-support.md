# Stakeholder and Support-Burden Analysis (ADI501)

> **Instructions:** For each stakeholder, describe a concrete failure mode when phone-matching fails — not a vague "confusion." Each cell should describe something that actually happens to that person.

| Stakeholder | How they use the tool | What happens when the phone-matching rule fails for them |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | *e.g., "reads the weekly narrative brief between orders to decide who to follow up with"* | *e.g., "may stop trusting the tool if a known regular customer is never flagged, without knowing why"* |
| Counter staff | *e.g., "asks customers for their phone number at checkout to enable tracking"* | *e.g., "has no way to know a typo they made at checkout is the reason a regular customer 'disappeared' from the system"* |
| Kitchen lead | *e.g., "sees the capacity warning and adjusts catering prep schedules"* | *e.g., "is not directly affected by phone-matching failures, but would be affected if a demand-shaping suggestion is wrong due to miscounted historical traffic"* |
